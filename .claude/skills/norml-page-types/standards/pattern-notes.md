# Generalized page-family patterns

These are reusable design patterns abstracted from the internal catalog. Named
client examples and live-project observations are deliberately excluded. A member
specification is a planning contract, not proof that its proposed routes or behavior
exist on the user's website. Inspect their actual target before making that claim.

## Distinctions preserved

- External providers, experts, staff, partners and brands have different relationships.
- A recurring owned program differs from a third-party course listing.
- Disciplines/specialties can be rich taxonomy pages listing multiple offers.
- Product application, product category, brand, compatibility and curated selection
  are separate classifications; keep one canonical product identity.
- Saved filter presets need a maintained predicate, unique visitor purpose and
  canonical policy; overlapping routes are unresolved until reviewed.
- Concepts and before/after galleries need honest delivered/concept status.
- Account, preferences, reviews, cart, checkout and builders have functional states;
  their presence does not prove formulas, purchase processing or notifications work.
- Installable resources have version/install/source requirements separate from price.
- A language is a representation of an entity, not an extra multiplication axis.

## Evidence scope

Navigation and XML inventories are discovery leads. Inspect final response,
canonical, page body and relationships to establish an observed role. Do not submit
forms, add cart items, purchase or change public state just to classify a page.
Keep target observations and URLs in the user's project record. Distinguish
observed, approved-planned and proposed pages; missing facts remain unresolved.
