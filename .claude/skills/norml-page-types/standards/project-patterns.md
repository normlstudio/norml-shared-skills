# Extract reusable page patterns from projects

Read this with architecture.md when extending from client/project evidence.
Use the existing family before adding another name for the same visitor job.

## Source and abstraction

1. Resolve the real project/domain from its context; dictated names can vary.
2. Read public navigation and available XML inventories for discovery.
3. Inspect representative hub/detail/child pages, final response, headings,
   canonical destinations and relevant relationships. Do not equate an XML
   entry, menu label or database taxonomy with a working content destination.
4. Compare the actual content model and visitor purpose with the catalog.
   Add a new family for an independent entity/purpose/lifecycle; add a member
   or variant when it belongs to an existing family.
5. Keep the reusable specification generic. Record dated public-source URLs,
   interpretation and limits in a static research note; current datasets,
   full private snapshots, accounts and credentials remain outside the package.
6. Label every supported variant that was not observed, especially optional
   root archives and internal singles. Report presence separately from quality
   and functional acceptance. Never submit forms, change a cart or purchase
   merely to establish a page pattern.

## Distinctions that the reviewed projects require

- External provider, external expert, owner’s staff, partner and brand are
  different entity relationships. Shared identity does not imply shared status.
- An owned recurring program is distinct from a third-party course directory.
- A discipline/specialty single may be a rich taxonomy page listing many offers.
  Category membership can differ from native term parent and from URL nesting.
- A product application is a use classification; a category describes the item,
  a brand identifies an organization/label, compatibility constrains fit, and a
  curated listing selects items by a maintained predicate. Do not duplicate items.
- Saved presets can deserve named pages, but they may overlap subject landings.
  Review identity/query/intent and choose a canonical policy; self-canonicals on
  two observed routes are not proof that the distinction is healthy.
- A gallery may have embedded-only detail, external previews or a link to an
  existing case. Concepts do not become delivered work or measured proof.
- Account/preferences/review/cart/checkout/builder pages have system or journey
  states. They do not need CPT archives or speculative extra public URLs.
- Installable resources have package/version/install semantics; a commercial
  product and a resource page should not fork the same offering identity.

## Locale across every family

Language is a representation dimension of a canonical entity/page, not a new
family or a service × city × language business entity. Record locale, original
identity, translation relationship, canonical route per locale and verified
language-switch/alternate links when applicable. Do not infer bilingual content
from a menu label or fabricate translated variants.

Language variants do not prove translation completeness or hreflang correctness. Locale-aware breadcrumbs,
links and source ownership are decisions for the consuming project.

## Maintenance

Use add-page-type.md, its templates and index reconciliation. Reusable source
precedents live in standards/pattern-notes.md; link new member sources there.
Record unverified candidates without advertising them as observed page families.
WordPress mapping, actual blocks and implementation remain with their owners.
