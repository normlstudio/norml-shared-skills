# Comparison single

Family: [Comparisons and alternatives](comparisons.md).
Member ID: `comparisons/single`. Logical role: `intersection`.

## Purpose and fit

Help one audience evaluate two identified offerings for an evidenced decision.
The record owns the evaluation, not duplicated copies of both product records.

## Route, identity and parents

- Illustrative route: `/comparison/{offering}-vs-{alternative}/`.
- Identity: one unordered offering pair plus its documented decision scope.
- Navigation parent: comparison archive/hub or an approved existing index.
- Entity parents: both real offerings; external competitors may remain external.
- Native role: one comparison record in the verified CMS collection.

Do not create both A-versus-B and B-versus-A, or another near-identical blog
article. Resolve historical canonical ownership before publication/migration.

## Content responsibility

Record comparison criteria, source links and verification date, intended fit,
tradeoffs, unknowns and maintained factual differences. Dates and versions
matter for pricing, limits and availability. Different billing units must not
be flattened into a misleading cheapest-price claim. Keep shipped/planned and
source-reported/independently-tested evidence distinct. No invented score,
testimonial, affiliation or guaranteed outcome; admit a competitor's better fit
where sources support it. Define the review owner and refresh condition.

## Relationships and navigation

Return to the hub; reference canonical owned offering/plan pages and external
primary sources. Related comparisons share an evidenced visitor need; they
are not automatic combinations of every indexed offering.

## Eligibility, omission and variants

Apply standards/architecture.md's per-pair gate. Embed short evidence instead
of creating thin singles. Defer missing research. Public requires the project's
editorial gate; an authored draft remains private even when its template is live.
One-to-many alternative roundups stay editorial unless a separate reusable
member is explicitly defined. This pair contract does not silently cover them.

## Structure and implementation handoff

No dedicated comparison layout exists in norml-page-structures at this revision.
Its article elements can inform a project-approved comparison layout. Do not
claim the article catalog defines comparison criteria or required tables.
Hand pair identity, source owner, review state, canonical route, parents and
actual CMS model to sitemap planning and the implementation owner.
